# Order & Organize

**Order & Organize** is a BepInEx 5 mod for [Supermarket Together](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2590510/Supermarket_Together/) that helps you keep your store stocked and your backroom organized. It adds smart restocking tools, optional hands-free ordering, and category-based storage shelves — all through the game's normal systems, without touching your save file.

**Current version:** 0.5.0  
**Requires:** [BepInEx 5](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases) (5.x — not compatible with BepInEx 6)

---

## Feature Highlights

- **Smart Restock Button** — One-click button on the ordering computer that adds every low-stock product to your shopping list at once
- **Automatic Ordering** — Optional background system that scans inventory and purchases low-stock products for you
- **Category Storage Shelves** — Tag backroom shelves by product category so items stay organized and employees put stock in the right place
- **In-Game Settings** — Press F7 to open a live settings window and adjust every option without restarting the game

## Quick Start

1. Install [BepInEx 5](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases) into your Supermarket Together game folder
2. Place `OrderAndOrganize.dll` in `BepInEx\plugins\OrderAndOrganize\`
3. Launch the game and open the ordering computer — you should see the **Add Low Stock** button
4. Press **F7** anytime to open the settings window

For full step-by-step instructions, see the [Installation Guide](Installation-Guide.md).
